# Accessibility and phone-speed pass before launch (2026-10-13)

Branch `launch-a11y-perf`. Written by the Development thread. It was meant for `dev/` in the project files, but that mount was returning I/O errors, so it lives in the repo instead.

## How it was checked

- **axe-core 4.14** through Playwright (Chromium 1194), with the tags `wcag2a`, `wcag2aa`, `wcag21a`, `wcag21aa` and `wcag22aa`. That last tag covers 2.5.8 target size.
  - 35 screens, each in light and dark at 360 px and 1280 px.
  - Reduced motion was on, so fade-ins don't skew the contrast reading.
  - The theme was set with both `prefers-color-scheme` and `data-theme`.
- **Non-text contrast (1.4.11).** On every screen, the edge of each text field, select and drawn switch or check box was measured against what sits behind it.
- **Reflow (1.4.10).** Horizontal scroll was checked at 360 px on every screen, and again at 320 px.
- **Keyboard pass.** The audit tabbed through 32 screens × 4 modes, up to 120 stops each. At each stop it checked:
  - that the focus ring is drawn;
  - that the ring has 3:1 against what is behind it;
  - that the focused control isn't hidden under the sticky top bar or the phone tab bar (2.2, 2.4.11);
  - that there is no positive tabindex.
- **Focus after actions.** Enter was pressed on each non-destructive button on 27 screens, at both widths, to check that focus never falls to `<body>`.
- **Menus, popovers and modals.** For the avatar menu, streak details, map pitstop panel and Departures detail, the audit checked that focus moves in, that Tab stays inside the modal, and that Escape closes it and returns focus to the opener.
  - The e2e walk already asserts the same for the three modal `<dialog>`s: the Express apply preview, the job board candidate drawer and the Find people drawer. It passes.
- **Token contrast.** Every token pair in `src/app/globals.css` was checked in both themes (see the next section).

Scripts used (scratchpad, not committed): `audit.mjs`, `keys.mjs`, `actions.mjs`, `dialogs.mjs`, `contrast.mjs`, `perf.mjs`.

### Fixes

- **Control edges (1.4.11).** A new token, `--line-strong` (light `#7f8798`, dark `#666e80`, Tailwind `line-strong`), is used for:
  - text fields, selects and search boxes;
  - switch tracks;
  - the task check box;
  - the Departures application-stage circles.

  The selected visibility segment gets a `line-strong` ring. The token is on `/design`, in Color.
- **Board focus ring.** `[data-board] :focus-visible` uses `--board-ink` on the always-dark Departures board and plan card.
- **Focus not obscured (2.4.11).** `html` has `scroll-padding-top: 4.5rem`. Under 760 px it also has `scroll-padding-bottom: 4.5rem + safe area`, so tabbing scrolls focus clear of the sticky top bar and the phone tab bar.
- **Focus never drops to body (2.4.3).**
  - Gap rating and Mark done use `aria-disabled` with an early return, not `disabled`.
  - `Button` gets a `busy` prop, which sets `aria-disabled` and keeps focus. Its forms ignore a second submit. It is used on sign in, test sign in, finish sign-up, upload, waitlist, company set up and join.
  - The waitlist moves focus to its "You're on the list" note.
  - Departures moves focus to the tracker's first step after "Apply on company site", and back to the apply control after "I didn't apply".
- **Reflow (1.4.10).**
  - The public header wraps under 360 px.
  - On phones under 400 px the streak chip shows the number only. Its accessible name is unchanged: "12-day streak. Show details".
  - Under 360 px the app bar shows the logo mark without the wordmark.
- **Scrollable message lists** (Inbox, company chat) take `tabIndex={0}`, so keyboard users can scroll them (2.1.1).
- **Token rule.** The "Boarding now only" switch is ink when on, like every other switch. Cobalt means progress only.

### Left as is

- **The map's "still ahead" track** is drawn in `line` (1.3:1). The stations on it carry their own markers and text labels, and the key names the track. The information doesn't depend on seeing the faint track, and darkening it would change the prototype v2 map. *Decided by Development 2026-10-13, Milin can reverse.*
- **Unselected segment and pill buttons** have no edge of their own. Their text (muted, 5.5:1 or more) is the control, and the selected one is marked by its fill, its ring and `aria-pressed` or `aria-current`. *Decided by Development 2026-10-13, Milin can reverse.*

### Decisions

- Add a `line-strong` token for control edges instead of darkening `line` everywhere. Card edges and dividers keep their quiet look, and only things you can operate get a 3:1 edge. *Decided by Development 2026-10-13, Milin can reverse.*
- On phones under 400 px the streak chip shows the train icon and the number, not "-day streak". This keeps the top bar from scrolling sideways. The full words stay in the accessible name and in the details popover. *Decided by Development 2026-10-13, Milin can reverse.*
- Under 360 px the app bar shows the logo mark only. The link is still named "CareerMetro, Today". *Decided by Development 2026-10-13, Milin can reverse.*
- A button that is working keeps focus and reads as unavailable (`aria-disabled`, `busy`). It isn't `disabled`, because that drops focus. *Decided by Development 2026-10-13, Milin can reverse.*

## Performance (phone)

How it was measured:

- A production build (`next build`, then `next start`) against the e2e database, signed in as the walk's main candidate.
- A 360×780 mobile viewport, `Emulation.setCPUThrottlingRate 4`, and Slow 4G: 150 ms RTT, 1.6 Mbps down, 750 kbps up.
- A cold cache, taking the median of 3 runs.

First-load JS comes from `.next/diagnostics/route-bundle-stats.json` (gzip -9 of the listed chunks). The RSC payload is the client-navigation response (`RSC: 1`), uncompressed.

Run-to-run noise is about ±100 ms on the timings.

**All four routes are inside the budget.** LCP is 1.1–1.4 s against 2.5 s, even on this throttled profile.

What the profile showed:

- **JavaScript.**
  - Framework code takes about 800 ms of script time at 4× CPU: React DOM 330 ms, the Next runtime 310 ms, the Turbopack runtime 160 ms.
  - The app's own code on Market is about 40 ms.
- **Market's extra cost is style and layout, not script.**
  - Its DOM is about 1,170 elements against 290–550 on the other three: 63 tiles, the states table and four selects.
  - Its RSC payload is 108 KB raw, but only about 10 KB once compressed inside the 37 KB HTML.
- **Fonts.** The three fonts are about 91 KB, and Market adds latin-ext for the ₹ sign. Together that is about half of what JS weighs on the wire.

### Trims made (no behaviour change)

- **Certificate form, loaded when opened.** `LazyCertificateForm` (`next/dynamic`) on Today's ride card and the Map's prove pitstop.
  - It starts loading when the opener is pointed at or focused.
  - On Map it takes focus itself once loaded (`focusFirst`), as before.
- **Life line, loaded when picked.** `LifeSvg`, `LifeSheet` and `LifeLegend` load the first time "Life line" is picked.
  - Each lazy part sits in its own `<Suspense fallback={null}>`, so the toggle and the page stay mounted and focus stays put.
  - Without the boundary, focus dropped to body. The action check caught this, and it is fixed.
- **Upload form only where it shows.** Today sends the upload form only in the No resume state, so the returning and first-day pages don't carry it in their payload.

Result: Map's first-load JS is 2.2 KB gzip (8%) smaller. Today and Departures are unchanged within noise.

### Not done

- **Turning the Today, Map or Departures views into Server Components.**
  - They are interactive throughout: ticks, panels, filters and the apply tracker.
  - Splitting them would be a large refactor for a few KB, on routes already well under budget.
- **Thinning the Market tiles** (a shared tile class, or plain `<a>` for the hidden table links).
  - It saves about 1–2 KB compressed.
  - Plain links would lose the in-place navigation that keeps focus and scroll.
- **Not preloading Geist Mono.** It would take about 23 KB off the critical path, but labels would flash in the fallback font on slow links. That is a fidelity call for Milin.
- **Self-hosting fewer font weights.** This changes the type, so it is out of scope here.

*Decided by Development 2026-10-13, Milin can reverse:* the four phone routes meet the LCP budget. This pass kept to lazy-loading what opens on demand and changed nothing users see. Bigger trims wait until real usage data shows a slow screen.
