Every stop on the route

How the route gets built

Five stops, in order — because the order is the point. Each one only works because of what the last one found.

RESUME ST.

Upload your resume

You share where you are today — real experience, real title, real skills. No forms to fill in from scratch; the resume is the starting station.

SKILL GAP JN.

See your specific gap

CareerMetro reads your resume against your named target role in your actual market, and names the two or three concrete gaps — not a generic "improve your skills" note.

UPSKILL LINE

Follow a short, personal path

A focused upskilling path sized to the gap that was actually found — days or weeks, not a semester-long catalog you have to navigate yourself.

INTERVIEW YD.

Practice for the room you'll be in

AI-coached interview practice tuned to the role and the gap you closed — so the questions feel like the ones you'll actually get asked.

JOB MATCH TERM.

Match to roles you're ready for

Not every listing — the ones that fit where you stand right now, so the applications you send are ones you're actually positioned to win.

Two ways to ride

Built to reach people two ways

The individual product earns riders through long-tail "how to get X job" content — a real, provable channel. The team product sells alongside work most companies already budget for.

INDIVIDUAL PASS

For one rider, one route

Self-serve. You bring a resume and a target role, CareerMetro builds the route. Grows organically through search — "how to get a [role] job in [city]" content that ranks because it's genuinely specific.

  • Resume + target role in, personal route out
  • Grown through SEO and long-tail content, not paid spend
  • Priced for an individual job seeker
TEAM SHUTTLE

For a whole department

Sold to companies for employee upskilling — closer to an existing consulting motion than a new one. One dashboard shows skill gaps across a team, not just one resume at a time.

  • Aggregate skill-gap view across a team or department
  • Sold direct, alongside existing advisory relationships
  • Priced per seat, billed to L&D or HR budget
