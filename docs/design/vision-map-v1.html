Ground rules

What stays true as it grows

  • The core stays freeResume to Match, Library, resume builder and network cost nothing. No affiliate links.
  • Clean job dataPasted JDs, hand-written role profiles and public ATS feeds. No scraping of job portals.
  • Code decides, AI explainsGaps and paths come from a skill taxonomy. The model reads resumes and writes the why.
  • Consent firstCandidates know when they talk to an AI, agree to recording, and a person makes hiring calls.
  • Private by defaultResume text never goes into logs. Never store a full Aadhaar number, at most the last 4 digits.
  • Video when it paysAI video interviews wait until the cost per minute and candidate trust make sense.
  • Employers get proof, not claimsEvery skill on a profile shows its evidence: gaps closed, certifications checked, AI screen scores and documents verified.
  • Employers reach ready candidatesOnly people who chose to be found, matched to the role's skill profile and already BGV level 1 verified through our integrations, so first rounds take less time.

Recommended order

Finish Path, Practice and Match first, then add accounts and profile, since every other portal feature needs a logged-in user. Library and resume builder follow, then community. Elite and B2B come after the free product has real users.
