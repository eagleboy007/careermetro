Direction

Calm, clear and built around one line of progress.

A light, airy interface with one cobalt accent. That accent always means "your progress": the line you move along, the steps you finish, the gaps you close. Everything else stays quiet, so the same system works for a job seeker on a phone and an HR lead on a laptop.

  • One accent, one meaningCobalt marks progress only. Red, amber and green are kept for gap status.
  • Evidence before scoresEvery gap quotes the line of the resume it came from.
  • Same parts, two audiencesGap chips, bars and steps are shared by the personal and team views.

What this changes

Decisions in this concept

Light first, dark ready

Light suits long reading on phones in daylight. Every color has a dark value from day one.

Plain step names

Resume, Gaps, Path, Practice, Match. The progress line keeps the metro feel without the word play.

One component kit

Chips, bars, steps and the progress line serve both the personal app and the team dashboard.
