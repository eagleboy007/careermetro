# CareerMetro — Product Requirements (for Claude Code)

> Drop this file in the repo root. Reference it from `CLAUDE.md` with: `Read CAREERMETRO_REQUIREMENTS.md before any design or implementation work.`

## 1. Product summary

CareerMetro reads a person's actual resume and a named target role in the Indian job market, tells them their specific skill gaps, and gives them a short personalized upskilling path, AI interview practice, and role matches they are ready for.

**One-line positioning:** Naukri finds listings, LinkedIn Learning and upGrad/Coursera sell courses. CareerMetro connects the two: *resume + target role -> your gap -> shortest path to close it.*

**Brand metaphor:** a metro map. Every journey is a "route" made of "stations". The landing page prototype (`careermetro.html`) is the visual source of truth for tone, colors and metaphor.

## 2. Goals and non-goals

### Goals (MVP)
1. A job seeker uploads a resume, picks a target role, and in under 2 minutes sees their top 3-5 skill gaps with evidence from their own resume.
2. They get a short upskilling path (target: 2-6 weeks of effort) sized to those gaps.
3. They can practice AI-coached interview questions for the target role.
4. They can export an improved, ATS-friendly resume.
5. Waitlist and SEO content engine live from day one (the B2C growth channel).

### Non-goals (MVP)
- Hosting our own course content. We curate and link; we do not build a content library.
- A full job board. Job matching in MVP is role-profile matching, not listing aggregation.
- Native mobile apps. Responsive web only.
- Employer tools beyond posting jobs and handling applications made on CareerMetro (no ATS replacement; candidate search only over people who opt in, FR-41).

## 3. Users

| Persona | Need | Entry point |
|---|---|---|
| **Job seeker (B2C)**, India, 1-10 yrs experience, switching or upgrading roles | "What exactly am I missing for this job?" | Google search on "how to get X job" |
| **L&D / HR lead (B2B)** at a mid-size company | See skill gaps across a team and assign short paths | Direct sales, consulting relationships |

MVP builds for the job seeker. The employer side for jobs posted on CareerMetro comes next (section 5.10, Milin 2026-10-12). Team skill-gap features stay Phase 3.

## 4. Core user journey (the "route")

| Station | What happens | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **Resume St.** | Upload PDF/DOCX or paste text | Parsed, structured profile the user can correct |
| **Skill Gap Jn.** | Pick a target role (+ optional city, experience level) | Ranked gap list, each gap linked to evidence ("your resume shows X, role needs Y") |
| **Upskill Line** | System builds a short path | Ordered steps: skill, why it matters, 1-3 curated resources, time estimate, a small proof-of-skill task |
| **Interview Yd.** | AI interview practice for the role and the gaps | Question set, answer feedback, score trend |
| **Job Match Term.** | Match readiness to role profiles | Roles the user is ready for now and roles they are N weeks away from |

## 5. Functional requirements

### 5.1 Accounts and onboarding
- FR-1: Sign in with Google or an emailed 6-digit code (the email also carries a link). First sign-up asks for an "I am 18 or older" tick and records an account consent. Phone OTP (India) waits on TRAI DLT registration.
- FR-2: A first-time user can run one gap analysis before creating an account (reduces drop-off); results are saved on signup.
- FR-3: User can delete their account and all stored resume data from Account settings (see privacy). Delete asks the person to type DELETE and signs them out; the account is hidden and kept for 30 days so it can be restored by signing in, then the daily cleanup removes it and everything under it for good, including the sign-in identity. One audit line with no personal data is kept. Account settings also offers "Download your data" as one JSON file (built 2026-10-11). The account name is set once at sign-up and then locked, because a new resume joins the account only if it shows that name; Account settings shows it read-only and corrections go through a request from the Grievances page, which leaves a record (Milin, 2026-10-12). Account settings part 2 (2026-10-13, dev/account-settings-plan.md): a username for the profile link /u/<username> (unique ignoring case, reserved words refused, one change every 30 days, the old link redirects for 14 days), an optional headline, current and expected pay kept owner-only (never on a profile, never to companies, never logged), who can see the profile (everyone signed in, people on your line, or only you; only you until the person chooses) and which parts it shows, and notification choices stored for when email is on. Your plan shows Free during the beta and the Elite prices, with no billing.

### 5.2 Resume ingestion
- FR-4: Accept PDF, DOCX, plain text paste. Max 4 MB (Vercel caps function request bodies at 4.5 MB; changed from 5 MB on 2026-10-07). A PDF that takes more than 15 seconds to read is refused with a message (added 2026-10-11). "Try a sample resume" (added 2026-10-20, v44 gap item 3) opens a checked-in synthetic resume, already parsed, with no model call; it is labelled "Sample" on the confirm step, Me, the screening check, the Map, the Life line and Today, and the person's own resume takes over once they check it and see its gaps; after that the sample is refused ("You already have your own resume. Open it from your Map.", 2026-10-20, T114), and its Gaps page reads as not found. Its Gaps step never calls the model or spends the explanation budget: hand-written explanations checked in for the role it suggests (Data Analyst), used only while each gap's state and resume line and the readiness numbers are the ones they were written for (an edited profile gets the template), the plain template for any other role (T113). Its path keeps template wording and reserves none of the path budget, and a pasted-job check against it counts toward 5 a day per network address, whatever the session. Its made-up jobs get no work-email check (T111), and its target role neither opens /u nor shows there (T112). The data download marks it `sample: true` (from version 32). It is never offered as a resume file, sent to a company or shown to others as the person's.
- FR-5: Extract into a structured profile: roles, employers, dates, skills, tools, education, certifications (with pass date and expiry when stated; the user can mark a certificate as not expiring), achievements.
- FR-6: Show the parsed profile and let the user fix mistakes before analysis. Never analyze silently on wrong parse.
- FR-7: Handle Indian resume conventions (CTC mentions, notice period, multi-column templates, Hindi/English mixed text).

### 5.3 Target role and market data
- FR-8: Target role is chosen from a curated **role profile library** (start with 30-50 high-demand India roles) or created by pasting a real job description. The library role is the ideal and its gaps stay the person's goals; a pasted posting is a per-job check on the Gaps page ("Check against a job") that shows what that one job asks for (covered, your goals it also asks for, only for this job, not asked, good to have) and never changes goals, the match, the analysis or the ride. The model only lists the skills the posting names with their exact words; plain code checks the words, maps them to the skill taxonomy and runs the matcher. The posting text is never stored or logged; 5 checks a day per person. A signed-in person can add an "Only for this job" skill as a "Your choice" goal tagged with the job (at most 20 open ones; the person can remove one, which skips it). Such goals are listed apart on Today and the Map, with their own learn and prove pitstops, and never change the train, arrival, match, readiness, gaps or analysis. "Plan for this job" opens the path with that job's open gaps first ("To apply here, close these first") without changing the stored path. Removing a check keeps its goals and drops the tag (2026-10-12, dev/role-suggest-plan.md part B). When the role isn't in the library, "My role isn't listed" lets a pasted posting stand in as the target: the Gaps page for that check shows the posting's required skills as gaps against the resume, decided by the same matcher, with a note that it stands in until the role is added; it makes no goals, path or match. A role search that finds nothing (only when the person presses Enter or clicks "My role isn't listed", never while typing), and the job title of a stand-in posting, are counted in `role_requests` (the normalised title and a count, nothing about the person; one count per person per title a day, at most 10 titles a day per person). Where a role was measured on public postings, each gap says how often they ask for it ("Asked in 7 of 10 postings"); other gaps show nothing rather than a guess (2026-10-12, part 3). On Today with no resume, target role chips above the upload box (Cyber Security Analyst, Data Analyst, Cloud Engineer, More roles) carry the picked role to the role step in the tab's sessionStorage, never the server or the address; the role step shows it first as "Your target role" until a role is opened, and the person can still pick any other (2026-10-20, v44 gap list item 1).
- FR-9: Each role profile holds: required skills, nice-to-have skills, tools, typical experience band, and a freshness date. A role can mark a set of required skills as "one of these" (for example AWS, Azure or GCP): the set counts as one required skill, is met by any one of them, and becomes one goal that proof of any of them fills (approved 2026-10-10, dev/role-one-of-plan.md).
- FR-10: Role profiles are refreshed on a schedule from licensed or permitted sources only (see open question OQ-1). Every profile shows "updated on <date>".

### 5.4 Skill-gap analysis
- FR-11: Compare the structured profile to the role profile and return gaps classified as *missing*, *weak evidence*, or *outdated*.
- FR-12: Every gap must cite evidence from the resume (or its absence) and the role requirement. No unexplained scores.
- FR-13: Provide an overall readiness indicator with a plain-language explanation, not a magic number.
- FR-14: Output is structured JSON validated against a schema, then rendered. Reject and retry malformed model output.

### 5.5 Upskilling path
- FR-15: Generate a path of at most 6 steps, sized to the user's stated weekly hours (default 5 hrs/week).
- FR-16: Each step: skill, reason tied to a gap, curated resources (prefer free and India-accessible), time estimate, one proof-of-skill task (mini project or exercise).
- FR-17: Resources come from a maintained catalog with link-health checks. The model selects from the catalog and never invents URLs.
- FR-18: User can mark steps done; path progress updates. Progress is kept when the weekly hours change. Readiness changes only once a step's proof task is checked (Practice), because a self-reported tick is not evidence.

### 5.5b Resume builder
- FR-19: Generate an improved resume draft from the parsed profile, rewriting bullets for the target role. Never fabricate experience or skills; anything the model adds as a suggestion is flagged for the user to confirm.
- FR-20: ATS-friendly single-column templates (2-3). Export to PDF and DOCX. First template built 2026-10-12: after confirming, the resume page offers PDF and Word downloads made only from the confirmed profile (no model writes any of it), with the account name when signed in; phone and email are never stored, so people add them in the Word file. The PDF font covers Latin letters, accents and ₹; a resume in Hindi or Tamil script gets the Word file instead (the PDF says so) until we embed fonts and shaping for Indian scripts. More templates come with FR-34.
- FR-34: Screening-readiness check (Milin, 2026-10-11; the holding site promises "AI-ready" resumes). Against the target role or a pasted job description, show: required keywords the resume is missing, layout that screening software reads badly (tables, columns, images, text in headers or footers) and non-standard section names. Keyword matching is plain code over the skill taxonomy. A missing keyword becomes a gap or a "confirm you have this" prompt; the check never writes text the person did not confirm. The fix-ups export through FR-20.
  - Built 2026-10-13 (dev/resume-check-plan.md): a "Screening check" section on the Gaps page (against the role) and on a pasted job's Gaps page, plus "I have this" on Missing rows of saved job checks. Missing keywords are the Gaps matcher's "missing" required skills (weak or outdated ones are named, so they are not missing keywords); "I have this" adds only the skill's taxonomy name to the confirmed profile, listed only, so it stays a weak gap and a goal; the server accepts only a missing keyword of the page's own role or job check, on the profile version the page showed. Layout (Word: tables, newspaper columns, pictures, text boxes, header or footer text beyond page numbers; PDF: text side by side and pictures, counted from the file's bytes without decoding any image), within their own time budget after the text checks, and section headings (whole-line match against fixed standard and renamed names) are read at upload while the file is in memory and kept only as counts, yes/no flags and codes in `resumes.screening` (migration 0025). Resumes uploaded earlier show "Upload again to check layout", never a guess. Readiness is words, never a score; layout and headings are neutral, only keywords use gap-status colour. The section links to the FR-20 PDF and Word download. No prompt change.

### 5.6 Interview practice
- FR-21: Generate role-specific questions weighted toward the user's gaps. Built 2026-10-15 as `/practice` (handoff 12h, dev/practice-match-plan.md), signed in only: the skill picker is the person's own goals (tagged current goal, goal ahead or goal met), each with 5 L1 questions from a question bank (`src/content/practice-questions.json`, 75 questions for the 15 skills role profiles most often require). The questions are content, never written by the model. Bank v1 is self-checked by Data research only, with no expert review yet; goals the bank doesn't cover yet are named on the page.
- FR-22: Text answers in MVP (voice later). Feedback on structure, specificity, and technical correctness, with a stronger sample answer. Built 2026-10-15: "Get feedback" (5 words or more) sends the answer to prompts/practice-feedback/v1, which returns words on structure, specificity and correctness and "A stronger answer" (a sample to learn from, not a script), checked by a Zod schema; no score anywhere, and nothing is scored by matching the bank's "what interviewers look for" line. 10 answers with feedback a person a day (India day), shown as "N of 10 answers left today", inside its own daily AI budget (`PRACTICE_DAILY_BUDGET_USD`, default $2) and a per-person cap of 20 calls; every call is reserved and logged in ai_calls with its prompt version and ids only. If feedback isn't available the answer is saved without it and uses none of the day's 10. Answers (`practice_answers`, migration 0030) are kept 30 days by the daily clean-up, are in the data download, go with the account and never reach a log. Practice never writes to goals or proof.
- FR-23: Track a per-skill confidence trend across sessions. Built 2026-10-15: after feedback, "How sure do you feel about this now?" (Not yet, A little, Fairly, Very) is saved on the answer and is the only input to the trend, shown beside the question and as "Practice confidence" on Me (one sparkline per skill, the latest rating and the session count). Private: hidden in View as others and on the public profile, never shown to companies.

### 5.7 Job matching (MVP scope)
- FR-24: Show which role profiles the user is ready for now, and which are within N weeks. Built 2026-10-15 as `/match` (handoff 12i, dev/practice-match-plan.md), signed in only, no AI: Ready now when every required skill is met or proved; Within N weeks from the path hours left on the role's open goals over the hours a week (3, 6 or 10, starting from the Today setting), rounded up, roles past 26 weeks left off. Plain code over the same matcher as the Gaps page; a role under a quarter of its required skills is left off except the target role. Hours: the stored path step where there is one (0 once done), else the Gaps page's hours table by status.
- FR-24a: Nearby lines (pending item 24a, built 2026-10-13, dev/nearby-lines-plan.md): on Me and as interchange stations on the Map, the live roles that share at least half their key skills with the person (at least two, at most 4 shown), with the share ("shares 4 of its 7 key skills with you"), the skills to add and the ones only listed on the resume. Plain code over the same matcher as the Gaps page; only skills shown in work or proved count as shared. Looking only: never changes goals, gaps, the match or the train.
- FR-24b: Market (handoff C6, approved by Milin 2026-10-12, built 2026-10-13, dev/market-plan.md): a signed-in `/market` screen, in the top nav and the avatar menu. Search by role, state or city; a tile map of India (one square per state or union territory) coloured in five plum steps by openings or median pay, with a list as its text alternative; the picked place's top roles, top cities and pay; the role's 12-month trend (Growing, Steady or Shrinking); pay bands by experience (middle half and median); top cities; the person's own Nearby lines (FR-24a); and the same role compared in two places. Figures use one row shape (role × state [× city] × experience band, openings, pay p25, median, p75, source, month), validated by a Zod schema. Below 15 openings a month a figure shows "Not enough data". Live counts from 2026-10-21 (Milin's D1, D3 (a) and D6; docs/plans/market-trends-plan.md): signed in, every figure is a count of open posts on public company job boards (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters; the daily Departures feed's `job_posts`), and the trend is new posts a month from `market_openings_monthly` (migration 0048), its first month dropped as a baseline; the header names the boards, the day they were read and the undercount note (only the companies on our list of public boards, skewed to tech and startups, most hiring in India goes through portals we don't read, Remote posts count in India only), and each card names its source with the day or the month counting began. Below 15 open posts a figure shows "Not enough data" (14 hidden, 15 shown); the trend says "Counting since {month}; the trend shows from {month + 13}" until it has 12 months, and needs each at 15 or more. If the counts can't be read, only that line and Nearby lines show, no figure. No pay on live counts: the pay card and the compare card say "Pay comes later: company job boards in India rarely state pay.", the place panel shows no pay, and the map's Median pay metric is hidden. The example table, labelled EXAMPLE DATA with its month on every card, stays for `/design` and the signed-out preview. No scraped or salary-site figures, ever. Nothing is stored; only role slugs and place names go in the address.
- FR-25: Real listings on Departures (2026-10-20, v44 gap items 40 and 42; docs/plans/departures-live-plan.md, approved by the Project manager under Milin's delegation). Roles come only from posts people paste, hand-written role profiles and the public job-board APIs of Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and SmartRecruiters, for the boards listed in `src/content/job-boards.json`, which changes by PR only; never scraped, never LinkedIn, Naukri or any site whose terms forbid it. A daily job (one GET a board, 15-second timeout, size and count caps, every answer checked) keeps posts in India or remote India, reads the live role from the title and the skills from the taxonomy by code (required under requirements-style headings, nice to have under "Nice to have", "Preferred" or "Bonus", all required with no headings), and stores no post text. Signed in, each row shows its fit by the same plain-code matcher as the pasted-job check, with no AI call; a post with fewer than 2 required skills found shows "Fit not shown". The header reads "Updated HH:MM" (India time, the last good run; the date after 36 hours). Saved and Applied (Applied, Heard back, Interview, Offer) are the person's own, kept on the server, 200 changes a day, at most 200 posts in each (a save or application past that is refused); Apply on company site opens the post and asks "Did you apply?". A post that leaves its board drops off All, stays in Saved and Applied as "Closed on the company's board" with no Apply, and is deleted with its saves 180 days later. In Download your data (version 26). Today's Departures board (handoff 5, v44 gap item 9, 2026-10-20) reads the same open posts and fit for a signed-in person with a resume: up to five rows, one per library role and city ("Pune · 12 posts"), ordered by the gaps of the closest post, with that post's gaps; posts with no fit shown, closed or matching no library role are left out; with no rows it says why. It is example data only signed out and with no resume, and shows "Job search is off" instead while the switch is off (FR-52).

### 5.8b Inbox
- FR-35: Inbox (handoff 12b; dev/inbox-plan.md, approved by Milin 2026-10-11). Direct chats and, with the employer side, one chat per application. Someone who isn't a connection sends one message as a request; nothing more until it is accepted, and "Delete" is never shown to the sender. Report (reason, optional note) and silent Block on every chat; reports carry the IT Rules 2021 deadlines (acknowledge in 24 hours; sexual content or impersonation in 24 hours; removal in 72 hours; other complaints in 15 days) and go to the grievance officer. Phone and email are never shown. New requests are capped at 20 a day per person. Message text never goes into logs or analytics. "Download your data" includes the person's own messages, blocks and reports; deleting an account for good removes its messages and the other side sees "This person has left CareerMetro". Part 2 (with the employer side): "Ask for times" with a slot picker and calendar free/busy. A person can turn off "Allow message requests from people who aren't connections" in Settings > Privacy (FR-55, on by default): while it is off, a non-connection's first message is refused on the server with the block's text, before the daily request limit is taken; connections, requests already received, application chats and company find requests are unaffected.

### 5.10 Employer side
- FR-37: Companies sign up with a verified work-email domain; the first person becomes Recruiter admin. Roles (Recruiter admin, Hiring HR, Interviewer) sit on the person's single personal account, are given by a Recruiter admin, and switch on after a 6-digit code to a work email on the company's domain. Removing the role ends access; the personal profile stays. At least two admins per company. Only verified companies post jobs or message.
- FR-38: Jobs, applications and a six-lane board (Applied, Shortlisted, L1 AI, L2 team, Offer, Not this time). Every move shows on the candidate's Arrivals; Not this time carries a note. Interviews, interviewer feedback and Ask for times: the candidate offers free slots and only those reach the company; the calendar is never on a profile. On Arrivals (v44 gap items 46 and 47), an accepted interview has Add to calendar: a calendar file made in the browser (UTC times, a UID from the interview, a reminder at 19:00 India time the evening before) holding the round, job title and company only, never the person's name, email or phone. Offers have their own group, empty until one lands; "N more applied, waiting to hear" shows once another application has moved; a Not this time role names a goal on the person's line, at its prove pitstop as the Map numbers it, only when the job asked for the skill that goal is on now (after a "Not for me" swap, the swapped-to skill) and it isn't proved or skipped. It says "Prove it and roles like this one open up.", never "Ask again": a job takes one application per person.
- FR-39: One company-owned conversation per application in the Inbox tables (FR-35); the job team writes, assigned interviewers read only, writing ends 30 days after closing.
- FR-40: Companies see Match to this job only (job requirements against public skills and accepted proofs, in words) and only applications made on CareerMetro. Never shown: email; phone until the candidate shares it at an accepted interview; pay; notice period (only Can join); goals, path, streak; other applications or interviews; an employee's own job search. Company access to an application ends 180 days after it closes; each view is audited (a board opening is one line). A company that isn't verified (suspended) sees no board, candidate, chat or phone, and suspending it erases every phone shared with it (security review 2026-10-13).
- FR-41: Let companies find me (pending item 19, 2026-10-13; dev/companies-find-me-plan.md). Opt-in only, off by default: a switch in Settings > Privacy, saved with a consent record. Recruiter admins (any open job) and Hiring HR (open jobs they are on) of a verified company search from one of their own open jobs, narrowed by any of experience bands, target roles, cities and verified profiles only, one or several of each together, and by a typed name, name + role, or name + role + current company (Milin 2026-10-15; a name finds the person even without one of the job's required skills; role matches the target role or a current job title, company a current employer, and job titles and employers only where the profile shows experience; typed words never go into logs, audit lines or the address) (T44, Milin 2026-10-15; an experience band matches only people whose profile shows experience, a city matches only people who gave that city, and verified means at least one skill proved by CareerMetro proof); Interviewers can't. A person may give an optional city in Settings, picked from the cities the Market map knows (no address); only companies that can find them see it, never the public profile, and it is in Download your data. Results are deterministic code over the skill taxonomy (Match to the job, proven skills first, at least one required skill), 20 a page, each person named by an opaque per-company id. Companies see the person's target role (T44, even with it off on the public profile) and city if given, the public profile parts the person switched on (never goals or gaps) and Match; never email, phone, pay, notice period, resume lines or the resume. Excluded: people who switched it off (at once), blocked the company or the searcher, work at the company (a member of it, or a current employer on the profile matching its name or legal name; Project manager's call under Milin's delegation, 2026-10-15), already applied to the job, or are deleting their account. Contact is one message request in a company-owned chat that the person accepts or deletes in their Inbox, under the existing limits. Every search, profile view and request writes an audit line naming the job only; searches are rate-limited per person and per company. The person sees which companies viewed them in the last 30 days and can block a company. Deleting the account removes views, blocks and found chats; the data download includes the switch, views and blocks. From FR-55 (2026-10-20), companies also see the places the person is open to work in, and a city filter matches the person's city or any place they listed; both only while the person is in search (switch on, job search on, none of the exclusions). An Express apply application carries the list as it was when sent.
- FR-42: Express apply, for jobs posted on CareerMetro only (pending item 16, 2026-10-13; dev/express-apply-plan.md). A job may set Express apply criteria: required skills that need accepted proof (chosen from its Required skills) and up to 3 certificates from our list; no DigiLocker, skill-check level, notice period or "gaps allowed" yet. The job's card on Departures shows each criterion by name, or as Met or Not yet for the signed-in person, from current accepted proof only (never the resume alone), in neutral styling since criteria are not gaps. Express apply opens a preview (a modal dialog) of exactly what the company receives: the public profile parts, Match to this job and the criteria, built by the same code as the company's drawer and checked by one strict schema; plus Can join and an optional note (300 characters, no email address or phone number). Unmet criteria warn and never block. Send in the preview, with the consent tick naming that one company, is the one tap that applies; consent, limits, audit lines, withdraw and the board are as in FR-38 to FR-40. Never sent: gaps, path, goals, pay, phone, email, notice period, resume lines. The company reads the note and the criteria (read live, so later proof shows) in the candidate drawer. Every other role keeps "Apply on company site". The note is in "Download your data" and is deleted with the account.
- FR-43: Verified certifications on the profile (pending item 13, 2026-10-13; dev/profile-certs-plan.md). Certifications come from accepted certificate proof and the resume; each shows its name, issuer, date and a status: "Verified with Credly" or "Added by you". The public profile (behind the Certifications switch) shows at most 5 current ones, verified first, then the most recent; expired ones are hidden there and marked on the owner's own profile. ## 7. AI requirements

- AI-1: Use the Claude API with structured (schema-constrained) outputs for parsing, gap analysis, path generation and interview feedback.
- AI-2: Ground gap analysis in the stored role profile and the user's parsed profile only; do not let the model assert market facts that are not in the role profile.
- AI-3: Prompts live in versioned files, not inline strings. Each has an eval set.
- AI-4: Build an **evaluation harness before launch**: 30+ real (consented or synthetic) resumes across roles, with expected gaps reviewed by a human domain expert. Track parse accuracy, gap precision/recall, and hallucination rate on every prompt or model change.
- AI-5: Hard rules: never invent employers, titles, dates, certifications, or resource URLs. Resume rewrites flag any added claim.
- AI-6: Cost control: cache role-profile context, cap tokens per analysis, per-user daily limits, and log cost per analysis.
- AI-7: Treat resume text as untrusted input (prompt injection, e.g. hidden "ignore previous instructions" text). Isolate it in the prompt and never let it trigger tool actions.

## 8. Security and privacy

Resumes are dense personal data (name, phone, employer history, sometimes salary and address). Treat the product as a security-sensitive system from day one.

- SEC-1: Comply with India's **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023**: clear consent at upload, purpose limitation, a grievance contact, deletion on request, breach-notification process. Confirm the exact obligations with counsel before launch.
- SEC-2: Encrypt in transit (TLS) and at rest. Resume files in private object storage with short-lived signed URLs.
- SEC-3: Store data in an India region where feasible; document any cross-border transfer (including to the LLM provider) in the privacy policy.
- SEC-4: Do not use user resumes to train models. State this plainly in the UI.
- SEC-5: Auto-delete uploaded raw files after N days (default 30); the user can delete earlier.
- SEC-6: Strip or mask phone, email and address before sending text to the LLM where the analysis does not need them.
- SEC-7: OWASP ASVS L1 baseline: rate limiting, upload validation (type, size, malware scan), CSRF protection, secure session handling, dependency scanning in CI, secrets in a vault not the repo. Abuse limits (sign-in codes: 5 per email and 20 per network address per hour; waitlist: 10 per address per hour; unreadable files: 30 per address per day) are counted in the database so every server shares them, store only keyed hashes, and let requests through if the database is unreachable. CSP violation reports (`/api/csp-report`) are capped per server in memory instead (100 log lines per address and about 1,000 in all an hour), so a public report never writes to the database.
- SEC-8: Role-based access; audit log for any admin access to user data.
- SEC-9: Pre-launch penetration test of upload, auth and admin paths.

## 13. Success metrics

- Activation: % of signups who see a gap analysis (target 60%).
- Perceived accuracy: % of users rating "these gaps are right" 4+ of 5 (target 75%).
- Completion: % starting a path who finish step 1 within 7 days.
- Growth: organic sessions from SEO pages; waitlist -> signup conversion.
- Quality: parse accuracy and gap precision/recall from the eval harness, tracked per release.
- Cost: AI cost per completed analysis.

## 14. Acceptance criteria (MVP)

- A new user can upload a resume, correct the parse, pick a role, and receive a gap analysis whose every gap cites resume evidence.
- The upskilling path contains only catalog resources with healthy links, within the user's weekly hours.
- Eval suite passes agreed thresholds (set in Phase 0) and runs in CI.
- Account deletion removes resume files, profiles and analyses within 24 hours.
- Lighthouse accessibility 95+ on the main screens; keyboard-only flow completes the full route.
- No resume content appears in logs or analytics.

## 15. Open questions (decide before Phase 1)

- **OQ-1: Market data source.** Where do role profiles and later listings come from: licensed data, partnerships, user-pasted job descriptions, or careful public sources? Scraping job boards likely violates their terms; do not build on it without legal review. This is the biggest product risk.
- **OQ-2: Monetization.** Free gap analysis plus paid path/interview pack, or subscription? Decide before building billing (Razorpay for India).
- **OQ-3: B2C first or B2B first?** The landing page shows both; engineering effort in Phase 1 should serve only one.
- **OQ-4: Domain experts.** Who reviews role profiles and eval labels so quality is real, not just plausible?
