Your next role, one pitstop at a time.

The mark is one metro line. It leaves where you are today, runs level while you get ready, climbs once you prove a skill, and arrives at a cobalt stop: your next role. It is the same journey the app draws for every person, reduced to a shape you can read at 16 pixels.

01 · Mark

How the mark is built

Drawn on a 48-unit grid with the 45 degree angle every metro map uses. One stroke weight, round ends, two stops.

{{MARK_GRID}}
  1. Where you are. An open ring: the origin stop, drawn in ink. It is where your resume puts you today.
  2. Getting ready. A level run. Courses and daily tasks move you along without changing your level.
  3. Proof. The 45 degree climb. In careermetro only proof (a skill check, a certificate, confirmed work) moves you up, and the line rises only here.
  4. Your next role. A solid cobalt stop. Cobalt means progress everywhere in the product, so it appears in the mark only at the destination.

06 · Voice

How careermetro talks

Like a friend who has done the commute before: plain, specific and calm. Metro words only where they help.

Rules

  • Say what to do next, in words. Readiness is never a score or a percentage.
  • Use the five step names as they are: Resume, Gaps, Path, Practice, Match.
  • Metro words that earn their place: line, pitstop, Departures, Arrivals, Timetable. Not every sentence needs one.
  • Never name or compare other job sites. Say "job portals".
  • Write for India: lakh, notice period, Pune, NPTEL. English first, Hindi later.

Say this, not that

07 · Care

Keep the mark intact

No status colours. Red and green mean gaps, not the brand.
No gradients, glows or shadows on the mark.
Never rotate or flip it. The line always climbs left to right.
Don't add stops, stars or extra lines. Two stops, one climb.

09 · Options

What else was tried

Three other directions, and why the line won.

{{ALT1}}

Route C

A "C" drawn as a progress ring with a stop at the end. Friendly, but it reads as a loading spinner and loses the metro.

{{ALT2}}

Interchange

Two lines crossing at a station, for community. Too busy at 16 px, and it says "network" more than "your next role".

{{ALT3}}

Station sign

A ring with a bar. Instantly "metro", but too close to a famous transit sign, which is a trademark risk. Ruled out.
