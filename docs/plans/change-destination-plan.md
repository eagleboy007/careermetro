## 1. What exists today (origin/main at 2411dcf)

How a role changes. No action or route sets "the destination". The destination is the role of `currentAnalysis`: the person's newest gap analysis by `created_at` (`src/lib/gaps/current.ts:17-30`). A person changes it from "Change your target role" (Map, `src/components/map/metro-map.tsx:667`; Me and Market `changeRoleHref`) or Match's "Gaps for this role" (`src/components/match/match-view.tsx:42`). Each leads to the role step and then to `/resume/[id]/gaps/[slug]` (`src/components/gaps/role-picker.tsx:75`). Opening the Gaps page for a role never analysed inserts a new analysis (`src/lib/gaps/store.ts:165`), and that analysis becomes current.

## 2. Changes, smallest first

1. **Coming back makes a role current again.** When the Gaps page reuses a stored analysis for a signed-in person and it is not their current one, it sets `result.chosenAt` from the database clock (`pickAgain` in `src/lib/gaps/store.ts`). This never happens for the sample resume or an anonymous visitor. `currentAnalysis` orders by `coalesce(chosenAt, created_at)` (`pickedAt` in `src/lib/gaps/current.ts`). A rebuilt older cut keeps `chosenAt` and `trackId` (`keepChoices`). Opening the current role writes nothing.
2. **The earlier destination.** `previousAnalysis` reads the analysis picked last for another role, from the latest profile versions of the person's own resumes (never the sample). `earlierLine` (`src/lib/map/previous.ts`, plain code) turns it into `MapLine.previous`: the role, the gaps the new line doesn't need (each with its proof and own-pitstop count) and how many gaps both lines share. `mapLineFor` adds one read, run in parallel with the others.
3. **Map.** `layoutLine` adds `earlier`: from the Gaps station the branch drops below the map (y 600), runs level to a closed end with one small stop per goal left there (at most 8), and is labelled "EARLIER LINE · title". The map grows from 560 to 648 to hold it, and the new line's stations, train and counts don't move. It is drawn in muted grey at half opacity, under the new line and never in cobalt. One button opens the "Earlier line · greyed out" panel. The panel says the gaps were worked out again and the new line branches off at Gaps, and that N goals are on both lines so their proof, learn ticks and own pitstops came along. It lists what was left on the earlier line (proved / N pitstops added by you, kept / kept) and offers "Switch back to {role}", which opens that role's Gaps page (change 1 makes it current). The key gains "Your earlier line, to {role} (greyed out)".
4. **/design.** "Change of destination" example (`exampleSwitchedMapLine`, built by the real `earlierLine`).

Built in the follow-up (section 5). Still later: the new line's learn ticks before Path is opened.

## 3. Data model

**No migration.** `chosenAt` is an optional field inside `gap_analyses.result` (jsonb), checked by the Zod `gapAnalysis` schema. This is the same pattern as `trackId` (`docs/plans/tracks-plan.md`). The earlier line is read from existing `gap_analyses`, `user_goals`, `gap_proofs` and `path_steps` rows. No table or column changes, and no index changes: a person has a handful of analyses, and the reads already filter by user. Download your data already carries `result` whole, so `chosenAt` shows there with no version change. To undo: drop the ordering on `chosenAt` and the field is ignored.

## 5. Follow-ups: the button, Hide earlier line, goals left behind (C25)

Design drew these as C25 (handoff section 7, prototype v53); **C25, Project manager approved on Milin's behalf, 2026-10-31**. All reversible, and still **no migration**. The handoff is copied into `docs/design/post-login-handoff.md`.

1. **"Make this my destination".** Opening a role's Gaps page (from Match "Gaps for this role", the Map's "Change your target role", Me, Market or the role step) no longer switches the destination. A new analysis for another role is stored with `result.notChosen: true` (optional jsonb field, Zod `gapAnalysis`; same pattern as `chosenAt`), and a stored one is reused without writing. `currentAnalysis`, `previousAnalysis`, company search (`currentResumes`) and Network (`analysesOf`) skip waiting analyses (`pickedOnly`) and order by `pickedAt`. The page ends on the card "Make this your destination?", two sentences, the primary button and "Looking from Match never switches on its own"; on the destination's own page the card says so instead, with no button. The button calls `chooseDestination` (`src/app/(app)/map/actions.ts`): signed in only, `{ analysisId }` checked by `destinationChoice` (strict, uuid), `makeDestination` (`src/lib/gaps/choose.ts`) under the person's Gaps lock, every well-formed try counted against `DESTINATION_CHANGES_PER_DAY` (20, `LIMITS.destinationChangesPerUserDay`), only for an analysis of the latest profile version of the person's own resume, never the sample's. It drops `notChosen` and sets `chosenAt` from the database clock (`pickedNow`). Nothing is logged but a failure's event name.
   - **When a page still picks on its own** (`picksSilently` in `src/lib/gaps/destination.ts`): the person has no destination yet (their first analysis, as before; asking them to press a button for the only line they have adds a step and nothing else), the destination is the sample resume's (their own resume takes over from the made-up person, as T114 already does), or the page is the destination's role on another resume (not a change of destination, so today's behaviour stays). Any other role waits for the button.
2. **The Map (C25).** The earlier line leaves at Gaps and runs below at a fixed depth in muted grey at half opacity (never cobalt or a status colour; its focus ring is ink), one stop per goal left behind labelled "LEFT HERE" and the goal name (14 characters; stops 120 apart), a short end bar and the mono name "{role} · earlier line · closed end". The destination label reads "NEXT DESTINATION · CHANGED {date}" (`previous.changedAt`, the current analysis's `chosenAt`; India time). The panel: kicker "Earlier line · closed end", the role, "You switched to {role} on {date}. Nothing you proved is lost.", "Came along to the new line" (proof kept, progress kept, kept) and "Stayed on this line" (proved, own pitstops, its pitstops kept), the carry rules in one paragraph, then "Switch back to {role}" (ghost; calls the same action with `previous.analysisId`, no dialog, and the other line is parked the same way) and "Hide earlier line" (quiet). "Hide earlier line" is also a toggle in the map head, only when an earlier line exists; hidden, a dotted stub at Gaps reads "Earlier line hidden" and tapping it shows the line again. Kept in client state for the visit only: no storage, no database. The key gains "Earlier line: the role you switched from".
   - Carry rules as built: proof is per skill, so proved goals keep their proof; goals keep their positions, so a goal both roles ask for stays where it was; the train is worked out from the line's goals, so the pitstop the person is on keeps its progress; the new role's goals are added at the end and the line draws only its own goals, so they take the places the left goals free. No positions are rewritten (a switch back would lose them).
3. **Goals left behind.** Derived, not stored: a gap goal (never a goal the person chose) whose skill isn't a gap of the current analysis waits on the earlier line (`leftOnEarlierLine`; `goalOnCurrentLine` is the same rule in SQL, reading each person's current analysis as `currentAnalysis` does). They stop counting in Library suggestions (`openGoalSkills`), the Timetable (matching and "for this goal"), "On your line right now" (other people's left goals too), Departures fit, Practice and Mock "Your gaps", and come back on a switch back. No goal status changes and no schema change; proof is never touched. With no current analysis every goal counts.
4. **Tests and walk.** `destination.test.ts` (the rule, the goal filter, `notChosen` and `destinationChoice`); `actions.test.ts` (signed out, refusals, refresh, log names only); `previous.test.ts` (`came`, `changedAt`, `analysisId`); `layout.test.ts` (labelled stops spaced and clipped, stub when hidden, new line unchanged); DB tests: A, then B waits, button switches, A offers the button, switch back; refusals for someone else's, an old version's and the sample's analysis, and the limit; on the Map, the left goals stop counting and come back with their rows unchanged. Walk step "change of destination": the card with axe light, dark after reload and 390 px; the Map unchanged until the button; the earlier line and panel with axe light, dark and 390 px; Hide earlier line, the stub, gone after reload; Switch back with no dialog.
